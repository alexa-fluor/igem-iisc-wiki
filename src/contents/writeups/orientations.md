## Fresher's / iGEM Orientation

With the current cycle coming to an end, we wanted to make sure the next MOSKATEER team could start their project without losing momentum, rather than beginning from scratch the way many teams do. To that end, we organised an orientation aimed at incoming students, where our team walked through what iGEM actually is, IISc's history of participation in the competition, how a typical iGEM cycle unfolds over the year, and — more practically — the proposed timetable and activities we had planned to get a new team up to speed, using MOSKATEER itself as a working example of what that process produces. We weren't entirely sure how much interest to expect going in, so it was genuinely enthralling to see over 100 future iGEMers turn up for the event, a turnout that said a lot about how much appetite there is at IISc for this kind of hands-on, project-based science.

What stood out most from the session wasn't just the turnout but the questions that followed — we were genuinely amazed at how wide-ranging they were, moving fluidly between the practical (how teams are formed, how much time it demands) and the scientific (why we'd chosen an RNAi-based approach over more established alternatives). Rather than leave it at a one-off information session, we actively encouraged attendees to start thinking beyond just joining an existing team, and to begin ideating their own project directions for upcoming iGEM cycles — treating the orientation as a starting point for the next generation's own ideas, not just a handover of ours.
