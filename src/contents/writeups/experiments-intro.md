## Introduction

Before running a single experiment, we spent considerable time simply planning what to test and why — a discipline we owe largely to extensive conversations with our mentors and PIs, who repeatedly pushed us to justify an experiment before designing it, rather than the other way around. Early on, our instinct was to jump straight to "does the delivery system work," but our mentors kept redirecting us toward smaller, more answerable questions first — ones whose outcome would actually tell us something useful, whichever way they went. That habit shaped how every experiment on this page is presented.

Out of those discussions, we settled on a consistent framework for how we plan and document each experiment, and we've kept that same structure throughout this page so that our reasoning is as visible as our results. For every experiment, we begin by **introducing the question** — explaining, in a few lines, why we were asking it in the first place, what gap in our understanding or design it was meant to close, and what we hoped the experiment would actually let us show. From there, we state **what we expected to see**, laid out as concretely as possible — often broken down into more specific sub-questions — so that an ambiguous or unexpected result could later be traced back to exactly which assumption needed revisiting, rather than leaving us guessing at what went wrong. Only after that groundwork is laid do we move into the experimental design itself: the rationale behind the chosen method, the materials and step-by-step protocol, and the controls and replicates needed to trust the result.

This approach paid off in ways we didn't fully appreciate until later. Framing each experiment as a direct answer to a stated question, rather than a generic step in a protocol, made troubleshooting far more targeted, and made it much easier to explain our work to people outside the project — including judges — since the logic behind each experiment is stated up front rather than left implicit. What follows is a record of each experiment we ran, written around the question it was designed to answer.
